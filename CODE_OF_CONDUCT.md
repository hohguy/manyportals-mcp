# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We promise to act and interact in ways that help build an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps this community:

- Being kind and considerate to other people
- Respecting differing opinions, points of view, and experiences
- Giving and accepting useful feedback with grace
- Taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from them
- Thinking about what is best for the community, not only for ourselves

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexual language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Insulting or derogatory comments, trolling, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a home address or an email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## A note specific to this project

ManyPortals holds credentials for several HubSpot portals, so reports and discussions often involve real business data. Two things follow.

**Do not include anyone's data in an issue, a discussion, or a pull request.** Not your own clients' data and not your own. Use the placeholders the project already uses: `PORTAL_A`, `example.com`, `123456789`. This is in [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md) as a contribution rule, and it is here as a conduct rule, because publishing a third party's records harms people who never chose to take part.

**A security problem is not a public issue.** Report it privately, as [SECURITY](SECURITY.md) describes. Posting a working attack against other people's portals before a fix exists is treated as a conduct matter, not only a process one.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take fair corrective action in response to any behaviour judged inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

The maintainer has the right and the responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and will explain the reasons for a moderation decision when it is appropriate to do so.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, and also when someone is representing the project in public spaces, such as using an official email address or acting as an appointed representative at an event.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to <manyportals@hohguy.com>.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer must respect the privacy and security of whoever reports an incident.

This project is maintained by one person, which has a consequence worth stating plainly: if your report concerns the maintainer, there is no second person here to escalate to. In that case GitHub's own [reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) are the route that does not depend on us.
